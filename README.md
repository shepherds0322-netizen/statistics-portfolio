### BCG Vaccination Rates in Portuguese-Speaking Countries
Tools: R (tidyverse, plotly?)

Conducted a statistical analysis examining Bacillus Calmette-Guérin (BCG) vaccination coverage and tuberculosis incidence across Portuguese-speaking countries using an API from the World Health Organization Global Health Observatory. Applied ANOVA, confidence intervals, and linear regression techniques to evaluate differences in vaccination trends over time and explore relationships between immunization coverage and TB incidence rates. Found significant variation in BCG vaccination growth rates across countries and identified an overall negative association between vaccination coverage and TB incidence. 

### CDC County-Level Analysis of Mental Health Risk Factors
Tools: R (Python? SQL?)

Used CDC PLACES data to analyze county-level predictors of poor mental health outcomes across the United States. Applied multiple linear regression modeling to examine relationships between health behaviors, social determinants of health, and frequent mental distress, identifying cigarette smoking prevalence as a significant predictor.
